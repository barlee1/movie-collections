# Rewind Club Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 27, 2026_

Rewind Club helps you keep track of the movies and shows you own or stream, and what you thought of them. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

- Your shelf, reviews, family members, profile name, and photo are stored **only on your device**.
- "Sign in" saves your name and profile on your device. There are **no online accounts** yet, and no ads, analytics, or tracking.
- When you search for a movie, the search text is sent to The Movie Database (TMDB) to find matches.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your iPhone only:

- Your name and an optional profile photo that you choose.
- The streaming services you select.
- Your shelf: titles, formats, services, watch dates, ratings, and reviews.
- Family members you add: their names, optional ages, who has seen which titles, and their ratings. Family information is never included in exports or shared, even if social features are added later.

This information isn't sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Movie search (TMDB)

To find movie details, the app sends what you type, or the text read from a movie case, to the [TMDB API](https://www.themoviedb.org). TMDB receives that search text and your device's IP address, like any web request. When you open a movie you stream, the app also asks TMDB where that movie can be bought, sending the movie's TMDB number and your country (for example "US"). It doesn't receive your name, photo, shelf, or reviews. Where-to-buy data comes from JustWatch through TMDB. The "Own it instead" links open eBay or a store's website in your browser, and what you do there is covered by that site's privacy policy. TMDB's handling of requests is covered by the [TMDB privacy policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy).

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

## Camera and photos

- **Camera:** used only to read the title on a movie case. The text is recognized on your device, and no images are saved or uploaded.
- **Photos:** used only when you pick a profile photo or a picture of a case. The app reads only the photo you choose.

## Files you import

Spreadsheets and Letterboxd exports you import are read on your device to add or update titles. The files aren't uploaded.

## Children

Rewind Club isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If the app adds features that share information, such as following friends, this policy will be updated before that feature ships.
