# Rewind Club

Keep track of the movies and shows you own or stream, what you've watched, and what you thought of them. Then let Tonight pick something that fits the mood.

## Support

Rewind Club is in beta. If something isn't working or you have an idea:

- **In TestFlight:** take a screenshot in the app and tap **Share Beta Feedback**, or open TestFlight and tap **Send Beta Feedback**.
- **In the app:** go to **Profile › About › Send feedback**.

## Common questions

**Where is my shelf saved?**
On your iPhone. Your shelf, reviews, and profile aren't uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Do I need to sign in?**
No. You can add movies, rate them, and use Tonight right away. Signing in sets up your profile (name, photo, and streaming services). Accounts that back up and sync between devices are planned.

**Why can't it find a movie?**
Movie search uses The Movie Database (TMDB) and needs an internet connection. Offline, it searches a smaller built-in list. You can always type a title in by hand.
